Meridian University — Operation: Secure Semester

A hands-on incident simulation in network authentication, PKI, RADIUS, and 802.1X.

Your Assignment
Junior Systems Administrator — Network Security

It's August. Fall semester starts in three weeks. Meridian University just suffered an authentication breach over the summer, and they've hired you to fix it before students return. You'll inherit a flat-file password system, a half-finished RADIUS rollout, a rogue access point, and a board that wants answers.

Every decision and every command shapes what happens next. Mistakes have consequences — and you'll have to live with them.

Academic Integrity & Honor Pledge

This simulation is your individual work for credit. By proceeding you affirm that:

  • The decisions and commands you enter are your own independent work.
  • You will not share answers, walkthroughs, or screenshots of solution steps with other students.
  • You understand the final debrief report is a graded artifact tied to your name.

Simulation rules: Answers are final — once submitted, you cannot go back or reset. You have 3 free hints available across the entire simulation. Hints beyond 3 cost 1 point each from your final score.
